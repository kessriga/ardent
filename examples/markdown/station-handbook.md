# North Pier station handbook

The gauge hangs from the east ladder. Its marks show centimetres above gauge zero. The west ladder has an older gauge
with a different zero.

## Opening the station

1. Unlock the cabinet above the east ladder.
2. Check the gauge against the brass plate.
   - Look for a gap between the gauge and the plate.
   - Read the stamped number.
     - A single punch marks the current plate.
     - Two punches mark the spare.
3. Copy the time from the station clock.

### Reading the gauge

Read three wave crests from the same step. Write down the middle value after sorting the three readings.

| Time (UTC) | Crests (cm) | Middle (cm) |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| 06:10      | 81, 83, 82  | 82          |
| 06:20      | 85, 84, 89  | 85          |

#### When the marks disappear

> At high tide the lowest rung vanishes first. If you lose sight of the red stripe, record a gap and return at low tide.

### Handoff

Leave the cabinet key on the blue hook. Enter the last reading in the [shift log](shift-log.md).

## Closing the station

Bring the logbook inside before the evening spray reaches the landing.

### Handoff

Leave the page open at the first blank row. The next observer needs the time even if you missed the last reading.
